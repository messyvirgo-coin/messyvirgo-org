# Style appendix for finance/crypto infographics

Append this paragraph after the infographic content description:

Style the graphic as abstract, understated, modern fintech UI—flat to semi-flat, soft edges, controlled glow, no fine textures or ornamental decoration. Use a dark chart base (deep indigo or night violet) with subtle neon accents in blue, pink, and violet, plus soft amber or yellow highlights that complement but do not overpower; keep overall brightness low and moody. Candlesticks must follow standard market convention: green for upward moves (close above open), red for downward (close below open), with clear bodies and wicks in proper OHLC form—no inverted colors, monochrome candles, or decorative styling. Charts must appear only diegetically on real in-world surfaces (screens, LED tickers, billboards, posters, glass reflections, or projection from a visible device)—never as floating HUD panels, corner badges, watermarks, currency icons, or random logos and text. Avoid symbol-heavy finance UI (no dollar signs, percent signs, ticker strings, or large numeric labels); if any text appears, keep it tiny and generic (e.g. “VOL”, “RISK”) and barely noticeable. Keep density low at the center, allow slightly more complexity toward the edges, and ensure the result reads as clean ambient detail rather than a dense dashboard or clip-art decoration.
